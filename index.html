Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the self-hosted social media scheduling application operated by Ruben Plata ("we", "our", "the App") at postiz.domusicnoexcuses.com collects, uses, and stores information when the App connects to third-party platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. The App is a single-user instance used exclusively by its owner to schedule and publish content to social media accounts owned by the operator.

1. Information We Collect

  • OAuth tokens and account identifiers obtained from TikTok, Instagram, Meta, and YouTube when the operator authorizes the App to access their own accounts.
  • Media files (videos and images) that the operator uploads to the App for the purpose of scheduling publication.
  • Post content (captions, descriptions, hashtags) provided by the operator.
  • Scheduling metadata (publish date, target platform, target account).

2. How We Use the Information

Information is used solely to publish the operator's own content to their own social media accounts, as authorized by them. The App does not collect or process any data from end-users other than the operator. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

3. How We Store the Information

All data is stored locally on the operator's own server inside Docker containers (PostgreSQL database and local file system). Communications between the App and third-party APIs (TikTok, Meta, YouTube) use HTTPS. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and used only to make API calls authorized by the operator.

4. Data Sharing

The App shares data only with the third-party platforms (TikTok, Instagram, Meta, YouTube) and only to the extent necessary to publish the operator's authorized content. We do not share data with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The operator may delete scheduled posts, uploaded media, and OAuth tokens at any time through the App interface. Disconnecting an integration revokes the App's access to that account. The operator may permanently delete all data by stopping the Docker containers and removing the associated volumes.

6. Third-Party Services

This App interacts with the official APIs of:

  • TikTok — TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Meta (Instagram / Facebook Graph) — Meta Privacy Policy
  • YouTube (Google) — Google Privacy Policy

Each platform's own privacy policy governs how those services process data sent to them.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
